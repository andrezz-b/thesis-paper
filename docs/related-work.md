# Related work

Papers relevant to the thesis, with full titles and links. **Verified** means the
title and link were checked online (date given); **unverified** means added from
memory, so check it before citing. Add BibTeX to `thesis/bibliography.bib` only when
citing. Papers that were read in detail have a note in [`literature/`](literature/README.md); the `thesis-description` skill links its row to the note.

## Knowledge-based visual question answering over Wikidata (closest work)

These pair images with questions answered from Wikidata or Wikipedia. Whether any of
them provides executable gold SPARQL is what [LIT-01](questions/LIT-01-existing-image-sparql-benchmarks.md) checks.

| Paper | Venue | Link | Relevance | Verified |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M³-VQA: A Benchmark for Multimodal, Multi-Entity, Multi-Hop Visual Question Answering (Ma et al.) | ACL 2026 (main) | https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25122 ([note](literature/ma2026m3.md)) | Multi-entity, multi-hop; entities linked to Wikidata, SPARQL used to build parallel questions, but the released gold is Wikipedia evidence (no SPARQL, no QIDs in the released data); evaluates agentic retrieval. Closest so far, contrasts with our gold SPARQL. | 2026-11-07 |
| ReasonVQA: A Multi-hop Reasoning Benchmark with Structural Knowledge for Visual Question Answering (Tran et al.) | ICCV 2025 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.16403 ([note](literature/tran2025reasonvqa.md)) | 1–3 hop questions from Wikidata for Visual Genome and Google Landmarks images, built by templates; released items are question + answer (open-ended or multiple choice), no gold SPARQL, no image index, plain vision-language models evaluated; code repository empty on 2026-11-07. Contrasts with our gold SPARQL. | 2026-11-07 |
| WikiVQABench: A Knowledge-Grounded Visual Question Answering Benchmark from Wikipedia and Wikidata (Shbita et al.) | arXiv 2026 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21479 | LLM-generated, human-curated multiple-choice questions grounded in Wikidata triples | 2026-10-29 |
| KVQA: Knowledge-Aware Visual Question Answering (Shah et al.) | AAAI 2019 | https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v33i01.33018876 | Persons in images, Wikidata, multi-hop; reference images for visual entity linking. Gold SPARQL not released (checked in the paper); note: [`shah2019kvqa`](literature/shah2019kvqa.md) | 2026-11-07 |
| Can Pre-trained Vision and Language Models Answer Visual Information-Seeking Questions? (InfoSeek; Chen et al.) | EMNLP 2023 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.11713 | Questions generated from Wikidata triples about visual entities; shows visual entity recognition is the bottleneck | 2026-10-29 |
| Encyclopedic VQA: Visual questions about detailed properties of fine-grained categories (Mensink et al.) | ICCV 2023 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.09224 | Fine-grained entities, single- and two-hop, Wikipedia knowledge base | 2026-10-29 |
| ViQuAE, a Dataset for Knowledge-based Visual Question Answering about Named Entities (Lerner et al.) | SIGIR 2022 | https://doi.org/10.1145/3477495.3531753 | Named entities (persons, landmarks, products) in images, KB of Wikipedia articles | 2026-10-29 |
| FVQA: Fact-based Visual Question Answering (Wang et al.) | TPAMI 2018 | https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.05433 | Supporting facts as KB triples (mostly common-sense) | 2026-10-29 |
| OK-VQA: A Visual Question Answering Benchmark Requiring External Knowledge (Marino et al.) | CVPR 2019 | https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.00067 | Questions needing external, mostly common-sense, knowledge | unverified |
